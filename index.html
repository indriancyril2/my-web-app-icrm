How the forecast works

Q(t) = Q_base(t) + Q_recharge(t)

Initial baseflow Q₀ decays exponentially by k. Incoming forecast rain is routed into effective groundwater recharge using a 2-parameter Nash cascade delay kernel h(t). Recharge water enters groundwater storage and drains under the same k: each hour adds (1 − k1/24) of the arriving recharge to the flow, so the volume released can never exceed the volume of recharge, and the part still in storage at hour 72 is not shown.
